Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

The short version

Orma uncovers a map as you walk. To do that it records where you go, continuously, including while the app is closed. That location history is the core of the product — it is what draws your map — and it is stored on your device and in your account.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not run ads. Your map and photo pins are shared only with the friends you personally accept; the few things that reach other people (like a comment in a city community) are listed below, and only happen when you choose to do them.

We may publish or share aggregated, de-identified statistics — for example, how much of a city Orma users have explored in total — that cannot be used to identify you.

You can delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

Location — including in the background

This is the sensitive one, so it comes first.

  • What: precise GPS coordinates, recorded continuously, including when the app is in the background or your phone is locked.
  • Why: it is the entire feature. Walking somewhere clears the fog over that area of your map. With background location off, the app cannot do the one thing it exists to do.
  • How it is stored: as an accumulated “explored area” (a grid of map squares you have uncovered) attached to your account, a day-by-day record of newly explored ground for the last two weeks (for weekly stats), the cafes and places you have been near or claimed, and the coordinates attached to any photo, beacon or postcard you create. We do not upload a timestamped GPS trail of your movements.
  • Who can see it: you. Friends you have accepted can see your explored area, your photo pins, the cafes you have claimed, and your exploration totals on leaderboards and your weekly recap. You can hide your own map and photos at any time from the map’s layers control.
  • Cafes you claim first, and places you add: if you are the first explorer to claim a cafe, or you add a place to a city, your name and profile picture show on that place to every signed-in user, with the date. Your other claims (which cafes, and when) are visible to your friends and to members of that city’s community.
  • Turning it off: revoke location permission in your device Settings. The app keeps working; your map simply stops updating.
  • Off until you turn it on: nothing is recorded until you grant location permission in the system prompt, and background recording only after you choose “Always”.

Motion and activity

Used to detect when you are stationary so tracking can throttle down and save battery. Processed on your device by the location library. Not stored on our servers.

Your account

Email address (for sign-in), username, display name, an optional profile picture and bio, your hometown if you set one, and your date of birth (Orma is for adults; your birthday is visible only to you). On iPhone, at sign-up we also ask Apple whether you are an adult (your age range); we use the answer only to check eligibility and do not store it. We also record which version of our Terms and this policy you accepted, and when.

Your username, display name and profile picture are visible to other signed-in users so that friends can find you.

Photos you take

Photos you choose to capture in the app, together with the coordinates where they were taken, and any caption you write. Photos you attach to a community comment are visible to every signed-in user.

Things you post

Friend relationships, comments and likes, beacons and postcards, community memberships, meetup RSVPs, and “explorers’ thoughts” left on places. Comments and thoughts posted in a community are visible to all signed-in users of the app.

Notifications

If you allow them, a push notification token so we can tell you when a friend sends you something.

Crash reports

Diagnostic data when the app crashes, so it can be fixed. This can include device model, OS version and a stack trace.

How you use the app

Product analytics: which screens you open and which features you use (for example, opening the feed, posting a photo, adding a friend), along with device type, app version and summary statistics about how well tracking is working (such as how many hours it ran and how many gaps it had). This is linked to your account and used only to understand and improve Orma. It is not used for advertising and not combined with data from other companies. You can turn it off in Settings.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. If that ever changed, it would only be with your explicit, opt-in consent — never by changing this page alone.

Where data is stored: these providers may store and process data outside your province or country, including in the United States, where it may be accessible to authorities under local law.

This website (orma.earth) uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It sets no cookies, does not fingerprint you, and does not track you across sites. It is separate from the app.

A note on how photos are served

Photos are stored with unguessable random filenames and served over HTTPS from public URLs, so the app can display them quickly. This means anyone who obtains the full URL of a photo can view it, even without an account. The URLs are not listed or discoverable anywhere, and cannot be browsed or enumerated.

We are telling you this because it is true, not because it is ideal. If you would not want a photo seen by someone who somehow obtained its link, do not post it.

How long we keep things

As long as your account exists. Delete your account and it goes. Copies may remain in our providers’ encrypted backups and logs for up to 30 days before they are overwritten; crash reports and analytics events are kept for up to 12 months.

Deleting your account

In the app, open your profile (You), scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.

This removes your profile, explored areas, photos, comments, beacons, postcards, friendships, community posts and notification tokens, and deletes your stored image files. It is immediate and cannot be undone. Local data on your device is cleared at the same time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. The delete-account button covers deletion in full. For anything else, email us and we will action it.

If something goes wrong: if a breach of our security creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will notify you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (and, if you live in Quebec, the Commission d’accès à l’information) as soon as feasible, and we keep a record of every breach.

Legal bases, where UK/EU GDPR applies: consent for location, camera, photos and notifications (each requested explicitly, each revocable in your device Settings), and legitimate interests for security, abuse prevention and crash diagnostics.

Age

Orma is only for adults aged 18 and over. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up and do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, email us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and notify you in the app before the change applies to you. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent.

Language

This policy is available in French and English. Both versions are equally valid.
